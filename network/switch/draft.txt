No gateway, no routing
Switches Only — never propagated to ER605
Accessible only via physical ports 6, 7, 8
Does not appear as routable destination in Switch ACL

Traffic Flow

AC750 AP sends untagged traffic from both SSIDs out of uplink
Untagged frames enter Port 3 → switch tags as VLAN 5
All management devices (Proxmox, NAS) use static IPs in 10.0.5.0/24
Mac Mini reaches VLAN 5 via ER605 routing (via LAG ports 2+1 — pending)
No DHCP — unexpected devices get no IP and cannot communicate


Why No Per-SSID VLAN Separation
AC750 is a consumer AP with no VLAN tagging support per SSID. All SSIDs share the same untagged uplink. Traffic separation between Dev mgmt and Prod mgmt is handled by:

Static IP assignment per device
ACL rule permitting only Mac Mini IP to reach 10.0.5.0/24 (pending)
AP client isolation
