DR UPS Monitor - Quick Setup on Proxmox
========================================

1. Copy dr_ups_monitor.sh to Proxmox:
   scp dr_ups_monitor.sh root@proxmox:/root/scripts/

2. Make executable:
   chmod +x /root/scripts/dr_ups_monitor.sh

3. Add cron (runs every 5 min):
   (crontab -l 2>/dev/null; echo "*/5 * * * * /root/scripts/dr_ups_monitor.sh >> /var/log/dr_ups.log 2>&1") | crontab -

4. Test:
   /root/scripts/dr_ups_monitor.sh
